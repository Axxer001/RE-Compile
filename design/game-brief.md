**Genre:** Aimed action roguelite (first person), at the current enemy scale, not a horde bullet heaven [Sir, 2026-11-15]

**Controls:** WASD move, mouse look, LMB basic, RMB special, E/Q skills, 1-3 spells, P passive, Tab Grimoire and Player ID, T effects, F interact.

## First minute
Start at the camp with Scorch and Burn and Cultivate. Walk the path north, meet Blighted Sprites, fireball them. *(INTENT UNKNOWN — inferred from implementation, not confirmed)*

## Challenge
Sprites patrol and chase by sight and sound; up to 20 are alive. Dying loses level, EXP, stats and chant tiers.

## Core loop
- Explore the forest, find pages, solve each page's Code Assembly puzzle to unlock it.
- Equip abilities, kill Sprites for EXP and levels, chant to raise stats.
- Reach rank D through the mission; on death restart with pages and rank kept.

## Goal & fail state
- Goal: clear the rank E→D mission (20 kills); more ranks later.
- Fail: dying ends the run (level, EXP, stats and chant tiers lost; unlocked pages and rank kept).
